# AcluxPro — Guide & tips

The same guide is in the app: tap **?** on the start screen, **Guide & tips** in any settings menu, or **How to add a key** in Cloud AI settings.
See also the [feature list](FEATURES.md) and the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

## Cloud AI: add your API key
Cloud AI runs Topaz Labs' professional AI on fast servers through your own fal.ai account. It's optional — without it the AI runs on the phone.

1. Open [fal.ai](https://fal.ai/dashboard/keys) and sign up or log in.
2. Add credit to your fal.ai account ([Billing](https://fal.ai/dashboard/billing)). You pay fal.ai per use — e.g. about $0.10 per 10 s of 1080p video with Nyx Fast.
3. Go to **Dashboard → API Keys → Add key**, name it (e.g. "AcluxPro") and create it.
4. Copy the key (it's shown only once).
5. In AcluxPro open the settings gear (Capture, Photo editor or Video editor) → **CLOUD AI** → **Add your fal.ai key**.
6. Paste the key and tap **SAVE KEY**. It's stored encrypted on the phone and only sent to fal.ai.
7. Turn on **Photos in the cloud** (Topaz upscale + Topaz Denoise) and/or **Video in the cloud** (Topaz Nyx noise reduction).
8. Optional: pick the photo noise strength and the video model (Nyx Fast is quickest and cheapest); keep **Wi-Fi only** on to avoid mobile data.
9. Use the AI features as usual — the AI badge shows upload, processing and download; **STOP** cancels. If the cloud isn't available, the app uses the on-phone AI and tells you why.

## AI on the phone
- **AI upscale**: Capture settings → AI UPSCALE → turn on "AI upscale sizes", then pick AI 24 / 36 / 48 MP under SIZE.
- **AI noise reduction**: Photo editor → Detail, or Video editor → Color → Detail → AI NOISE REDUCTION (tap ⓘ for timing info).
- Video AI noise reduction on the phone can take several minutes up to about an hour — Cloud AI does it in minutes.
- Tap **STOP** on the AI badge at any time.

## Capture tips
- Volume keys work as shutter / record.
- PRO and PRO VIDEO give manual ISO, shutter, focus, white balance and exposure.
- Zebras, false colour and focus peaking help judge exposure and focus.
- H.265 = smaller files at the same quality; H.264 plays everywhere.
- D-Log / D-Log M / Flat keep more dynamic range — grade later in the Video editor (Color → Input).
- Clean feed hides the controls for screen mirroring / HDMI.
- In LIVE, add your stream key once per platform; choose H.264 if your platform doesn't accept H.265.

## Photo editor tips
- Hold your finger on the photo to see the original.
- Sliders update live while you drag.
- COPY / PASTE SETTINGS moves a look to another photo — or to a video clip.
- SAVE keeps the edit as a project; LOAD opens it again.
- Hold the divider, then drag to resize panels; double-tap to reset.
- "Remember watermark" adds your watermark to every photo automatically.

## Video editor tips
- Pinch the timeline to zoom; the scissors under the player split at the playhead.
- Tap a cut on the timeline to choose one of 34 transitions.
- Edit → Crop & zoom: pinch, drag and twist on the player; Ken Burns animates start → end.
- Heavy clips (HDR, 10-bit, 4K) get a light preview copy for smooth playback; exports use the originals.
- If playback stalls, turn on "Show playback info" in settings and share a screenshot.
- Hold a divider to resize the player, timeline or controls; double-tap to reset.
- SAVE / LOAD projects; "Save & leave" when exiting.

## General
- Change the app colour in any settings menu (APP COLOUR).
- "Always start in …" opens the app straight into Capture, the Photo editor or the Video editor.
- Choose where photos and exports are saved (SAVE LOCATION in the editors' settings).
- The Exit button on the start screen closes the app completely.
