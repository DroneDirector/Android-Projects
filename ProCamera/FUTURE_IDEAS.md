# AlcorCut Pro — Future ideas

Possible features for later. **Not** on the active to-do list: nothing here is started until the owner asks.

## Capture
- **Gimbal control** (motor pan / tilt / tracking from the app). Needs the gimbal maker's developer kit and agreement:
  DJI has no public Android kit for Osmo Mobile gimbals (partner deals only); Zhiyun has offered a kit to app
  makers on request. Record start / stop from a gimbal's own button already works when the gimbal sends volume-key
  presses (Settings → Side buttons).
  Option B for DJI without a partner deal: own code from the observed Bluetooth protocol (EU interoperability rules),
  only if no lock / encryption has to be broken, with an IP lawyer's review first and an "unofficial" label — see
  Guides and Tips/AlcorCut-Pro-gimbal-support-notes.pdf, page 2.

## Photo editor — fresh ideas (2026-10-28, checked against FEATURES.md)
Rough cost: small ≈ 0.3–0.5 % of a Pro week, medium ≈ 1 %, large ≈ 2–3 %.
1. **Crowd removal & star trails** — stack several photos of the same scene: median (moving people vanish) or lighten (star trails). Medium.
2. **Face-aware liquify** — sliders for eye size, face width, chin, smile, using the face landmarks. Medium.
3. **Dodge & burn brushes** — dedicated lighten / darken painting with soft strength. Small.
4. **Colour splash** — tap a colour to keep it, everything else turns black & white. Small.
5. **Tilt-shift miniature** — a sharp band with blurred top and bottom and a little extra colour. Small.
6. **Double exposure** — blend a second photo inside the AI-found subject (silhouette). Medium.
7. **Sun glow & light rays** — paint in a sun glow with rays through trees or clouds. Small.
8. **Metadata editor** — title, caption, keywords, copyright and remove location, for one or many photos. Small.
9. **Slideshow to video** — selected photos become a video with slow zoom, music and transitions, opened in the video editor. Medium.
10. **Thumbnail / cover designer** — YouTube-style 16:9 cover: big outlined text, AI cut-out subject with glow. Medium.

## Video editor — fresh ideas (2026-10-28, checked against FEATURES.md)
1. **Text behind subject** — a title placed behind the AI-found person or object, moving with them. Large.
2. **Sky replacement for video** — the photo editor's sky swap, followed through the clip. Large.
3. **Vertical + horizontal in one export** — 16:9 and 9:16 (auto-reframed) from the same edit in one go. Small.
4. **YouTube chapters from markers** — a ready-to-paste chapter list and chapters inside the MP4. Small.
5. **Edit templates** — travel recap, drone reel, intro: drop clips in, cut to the beat with titles and transitions. Medium.
6. **Caption translation** — translate captions on the phone (no Faroese model exists; Danish and English do). Medium.
7. **Cover frame** — pick a frame (with an optional title) as the video's thumbnail inside the file. Small.
8. **Voice / music separation** — split a clip's sound into voice and music (AI, MIT-licensed model, large download). Large.
9. **Retro looks for whole clips** — VHS, CRT screen, camcorder date stamp. Small.
10. **Animated route map from GPX** — the flight-path overlay for hikes, drives and rides recorded with a GPS app. Small.
