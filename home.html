Capture

Why Capture is pro

The tools of a cinema camera, in your pocket.

  1. 01

    Full manual control

    ISO, shutter, focus, white balance and EV — with auto, shutter priority, ISO priority and manual exposure modes.

  2. 02

    Cinema monitoring

    Zebras, false colour, focus peaking, waveform, RGB parade, vectorscope, a focus magnifier and your own .cube monitoring LUT.

  3. 03

    Real cinema settings

    Cinema frame shapes (2.39, 1.85, 2.76…), shutter angle with one-tap 180°, NTSC rates, anamorphic desqueeze and A / B pulls for focus, zoom and exposure.

  4. 04

    Pro sound

    24-bit WAV, manual gain with limiter, headphone monitoring, stereo level meters and a wind filter.

  5. 05

    Built for the set

    Slate & shot log (CSV), custom set-ups C1–C3, remote monitor, clean feed and a Wear OS watch remote.

…and much more

Photo editor

Why the Photo editor is pro

From sensor data to print — without leaving your phone.

  1. 01

    Real RAW development

    Reads the sensor data itself — DNG and camera RAW from Sony, Canon, Nikon, Fujifilm, DJI and more — with RAW looks measured from each maker.

  2. 02

    Pro colour tools

    Tone curve, colour wheels, HSL mixer, point colour, calibration, and a targeted-adjustment tool you drag right on the photo.

  3. 03

    AI masks

    Sky, people (skin, eyes, hair, clothes…), objects and depth — combined with add, subtract and intersect.

  4. 04

    Retouching

    Healing, automatic dust-spot finding, AI object removal, liquify and frequency separation.

  5. 05

    Print-grade output

    TIFF, colour spaces, output sharpening, soft proofing with gamut warning, Ultra HDR, export presets and .xmp presets for other RAW editors.

…and much more

Video editor

Why the Video editor is pro

A real editing suite — not a clip trimmer.

  1. 01

    A real multi-track timeline

    Unlimited video layers, keyframes with Bézier curves, motion tracking, nesting, J- and L-cuts, and slip, roll and slide trims.

  2. 02

    Colour grading

    Colour wheels, curves, HSL, LUTs, masks and film & lens effects — with scopes.

  3. 03

    Pro workflow

    Proxies, multicam synced by sound, a render cache, project archives and version history.

  4. 04

    Pro audio

    Parametric EQ with de-esser and live spectrum, loudness normalisation (−14 / −16 / −23 LUFS), voiceover and beat markers.

  5. 05

    Pro delivery

    Background export and render queue, 4K with HDR kept, smart render, review copies with timecode, EDL / FCPXML / OpenTimelineIO and .srt / .vtt subtitles.

…and much more
